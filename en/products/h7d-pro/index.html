FlyingRC H7D Pro H743 FPV Flight Controller

A Pro H743 FPV flight controller for feature-rich multirotor builds. It performs the same central role as H7D but uses the Pro variant material and a switchable 9 V VTX/camera BEC.

FPV / multirotorFlight controllerSTM32H743PX4BetaflightArduPilotOnboard BECPWM outputs
Buy on Taobao Email sales WhatsApp support GitHub

Best used for

  • FPV builds requiring H743 performance with Pro-family layout.
  • Digital/analog video setups where switchable VTX power is useful.
  • Users who want dual gyro/barometer stability features.

Key features

  • STM32H743VIT6 MCU family with dual high-precision gyros and barometer.
  • Switchable onboard 9 V BEC controlled through PINIO1/User1.
  • BF, AP, INAV, and PX4 firmware paths are documented; factory default is BF4.4.2.

Setup / wiring notes

  • Use the H7D Pro wiring references before connecting video, receiver, and LED accessories.
  • Do not infer ArduPilot SERIAL numbers from UART labels without the mapping table.

Check before use

  • Choose the firmware path that matches your build, and verify the board target, wiring, and sensor orientation before flight.

Key specs

  • STM32H743 Pro FPV flight controller
  • Dual gyro and barometer sensor architecture
  • Switchable onboard 9 V BEC for VTX/camera power
