FlyingRC H7Wlite V2 H743 Fixed-wing Flight Controller

H7Wlite V2 hardware revision with dual BMI270 IMUs, SPA06-003 barometer, and internal QMC5883P compass. ArduPilot and INAV retain legacy ICM support; Betaflight is BMI270-only.

Fixed-wingFlight controllerSTM32H743ArduPilotINAVBetaflightCANPWM outputs
Buy on Taobao Email sales WhatsApp support GitHub

Key specs

  • STM32H743 fixed-wing flight controller
  • Dual BMI270 IMUs; legacy ICM alternatives remain available in ArduPilot and INAV
  • SPA06-003 barometer and internal QMC5883P compass on I2C2
  • 12 PWM outputs, SDIO logging, analog OSD, CAN, and seven UARTs
